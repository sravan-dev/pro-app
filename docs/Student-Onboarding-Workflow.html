Student Onboarding & Allocation Workflow

From admission to dual-support learning — teams, advising, tutoring & feedback
Process reference document
This workflow describes how a new student moves from admission through team allocation, advising, and into individual classes with a dual-support model, closing with a rating system for the people who serve them. Roles are colour-coded below.
Sales Team Team Manager Team Advisor Tutor Student
1
Phase One

Admission & Initial Team Allocation

  1. Sales Team
    Admission

    The Sales Team processes a new student admission.

  2. HubSpot
    HubSpot Intimation

    Admission data is pushed to HubSpot, which sends an intimation to the Team Managers and Team Advisors.

  3. Allocation
    Round-Robin Team Assignment

    Students are assigned to teams in round-robin order (Student 1 → Team 1, Student 2 → Team 2, … up to Team 4, then the cycle repeats).

  4. HubSpot
    Ticket Creation

    A ticket is automatically raised for the newly allocated student to initiate the enrollment process.

2
Phase Two

Onboarding & Advising

  1. Team
    Introductory Call

    The assigned team contacts the student for an intro call to explain course details, policies and criteria.

  2. Team Advisor
    Advisor Allocation

    A specific Team Advisor (from a pool of 3–4 advisors under the Team Manager) is allocated to the student.

  3. Team Advisor
    Primary Point of Contact

    The advisor owns the student's general care: feedback collection, follow-ups, and resolving issues.

  4. Student
    Common Classes

    The student begins by attending common, live online group classes.

3
Phase Three

Transition to Individual Classes

  1. Team Advisor
    Gathering Preferences

    When the student is ready for individual classes, the advisor collects preferences: preferred class time, male/female tutor preference, and fixed vs. flexible working hours.

  2. Team Advisor
    Manager Intimation

    The advisor sends the collected preferences to the Team Manager.

  3. Team Manager
    Tutor Allocation

    Based on the preferences, the Team Manager allocates an appropriate Tutor to the student.

  4. Tutor + Advisor
    Dual Support System

    The student now has two direct contacts: the Tutor (academic classes) and the Team Advisor (ongoing updates & support).

Feedback & Rating System

Students provide ratings for all three key people in their journey, keeping accountability and service quality visible to managers.

System support status. In the current LMS build, the following are implemented: HubSpot contact display & enrollment with e-mail intimation to managers/advisors, configurable Teams, manual assignment of student → team/advisor/tutor, role-scoped portals, and the student rating system (1–5 stars + comment for Manager, Advisor & Tutor). Planned / not yet automated: round-robin allocation, HubSpot ticket creation, and preference-based tutor matching. Per policy, HubSpot is read-only — the app never writes back to it.
